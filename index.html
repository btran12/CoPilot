CoPilot

Your co-pilot for the road

Mount your phone facing the road. CoPilot watches ahead and speaks up when something needs your attention, so you can keep your eyes where they belong.

  • Forward collision warning
  • Lane departure warning
  • Green-light nudge
  • Traffic light and stop / go awareness
  • Pedestrian, cyclist and stop-sign alerts
  • Emergency-vehicle detection Beta

A driving aid, not a self-driving system. Keep your eyes on the road, obey traffic laws, and never interact with the phone while driving. Camera, GPS and sensor data stay on this device.
